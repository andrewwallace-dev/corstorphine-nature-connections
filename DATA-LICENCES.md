## OpenStreetMap

© OpenStreetMap contributors. The OSM-derived mapping in data/habitats.geojson and data/evidence.geojson is made available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0.

- Copyright and attribution: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
- Licence: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
- Snapshot: 23 October 2026, using nine small OSM map API extracts.

Relevant ways touching the study area and complete relevant multipolygons were retained. Polygons may overlap and can extend beyond the study boundary. Mapped land cover is not an ecological quality, access or ownership assessment.

## City of Edinburgh Council

The community council boundary in data/boundary.geojson is from the City of Edinburgh Council’s Community Council Boundaries dataset, effective from 28 March 2025. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

- Licence: https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/
- Dataset: https://services-eu1.arcgis.com/FgpikkYuSUOuITxp/arcgis/rest/services/Community_Council_Boundaries_from_28_March_2025/FeatureServer/189

## Candidate connections

The eight candidate connections are manually interpreted, illustrative axes between mapped features. They are not official B-Lines, approved projects, surveyed routes or demonstrated wildlife movements. Their mapped anchors remain linked in the data. Read the Sources & method panel before using them for decisions.
